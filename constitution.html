Constitution of
Awam Pragati Party

The supreme governing document of Awam Pragati Party, establishing democratic values, constitutional governance, transparency, public service, national unity and responsible leadership.

Read Constitution

Preamble

We, the members of Awam Pragati Party (APP), unite ourselves to uphold the Constitution of India, strengthen democracy, promote equality, encourage public participation, protect national unity, and work for the progress and welfare of every citizen.

This Constitution establishes the organisational framework, rights, duties, leadership, principles and democratic procedures that shall govern the Party.

Articles

Article I — Name & Identity

Section 1 — Official Name

The official name of the organisation shall be Awam Pragati Party (APP).

Section 2 — Motto

जन की आवाज • राष्ट्र की प्रगति

Section 3 — Nature

Awam Pragati Party shall function as a democratic political organisation committed to constitutional governance, national unity, transparency, social justice, public participation and sustainable development.

Section 4 — Official Language

English and Hindi shall remain the principal working languages of the organisation. Regional languages may also be used whenever appropriate.

Article II — Fundamental Principles

  • Respect for the Constitution of India.
  • Democracy and rule of law.
  • Equality before every citizen.
  • No discrimination on religion, caste, language, gender or region.
  • Transparency and accountability.
  • Peaceful democratic participation.
  • Evidence-based policymaking.
  • National unity and inclusive development.

Article III — Objectives

  • Strengthen democratic participation.
  • Promote constitutional awareness.
  • Ensure transparent governance.
  • Promote education and employment.
  • Encourage innovation and technology.
  • Support sustainable development.
  • Protect national unity.
  • Promote equal opportunities for all citizens.

Article IV — Membership

Eligibility

  • Accept this Constitution.
  • Follow Party rules and discipline.
  • Respect democratic values.
  • Work towards public welfare.
  • Not be permanently removed for serious misconduct.

Rights

  • Participate in Party activities.
  • Vote whenever eligible.
  • Contest constitutional offices.
  • Submit suggestions and proposals.
  • Receive equal treatment without discrimination.

Duties

  • Respect fellow members.
  • Protect the reputation of APP.
  • Maintain discipline.
  • Promote constructive public dialogue.
  • Faithfully uphold this Constitution.

Article V — Constitutional Offices

The constitutional leadership of Awam Pragati Party (APP) shall consist of the following eight principal offices:

  1. President
  2. Vice-President
  3. Executive Director
  4. Deputy Chairperson
  5. Secretary-General
  6. Chief Whip
  7. Media Director
  8. Director of Administration

These offices shall collectively constitute the principal leadership structure of the Party. Each office shall exercise only those powers and responsibilities assigned to it under this Constitution.

Additional constitutional offices, committees or departments may be established through constitutional amendment whenever organisational requirements demand.

Article VI — President

The President shall be the highest constitutional authority of the Awam Pragati Party and shall safeguard the vision, principles, unity and Constitution of the Party.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Provide overall leadership to the Party.
  • Represent the Party at national and public forums.
  • Preside over meetings of the National Executive Council.
  • Approve major constitutional and organisational decisions.
  • Safeguard the Constitution and fundamental principles of the Party.
  • Promote unity, discipline, integrity and responsible leadership.
  • Provide constitutional guidance to all Party offices.
  • Ensure that the Party operates in accordance with this Constitution.

Constitutional Protection

The office of the President shall remain constitutionally protected and may only become vacant through voluntary resignation, permanent incapacity, removal through the constitutional process or another procedure specifically provided under this Constitution.

Article VII — Vice-President

The Vice-President shall assist the President in carrying out executive and constitutional functions and shall discharge the duties of the President whenever authorised or required.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Assist the President in Party leadership and administration.
  • Coordinate major national Party programmes.
  • Supervise committees assigned by the President.
  • Preside over constitutional meetings in the absence of the President.
  • Strengthen coordination among constitutional offices.
  • Assist in resolving organisational matters.
  • Support the implementation of decisions of the Party leadership.
  • Perform additional constitutional duties assigned by the President.

Acting President

Whenever the President is temporarily unable to perform the duties of office, the Vice-President may discharge those duties until the President resumes office or the constitutional procedure for succession is completed.

Article VIII — Executive Director

The Executive Director shall serve as the Chief Executive Officer of the Party and shall supervise its day-to-day administration, organisational operations and implementation of Party decisions.

Election

The Executive Director shall be elected every six months through a free, fair and transparent vote conducted among eligible members. A person may serve a maximum of two consecutive terms before becoming eligible again after one complete term.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Implement decisions of the Party leadership.
  • Coordinate all Party departments.
  • Supervise day-to-day Party administration.
  • Monitor implementation of Party programmes.
  • Coordinate organisational activities and campaigns.
  • Ensure cooperation between constitutional offices.
  • Present organisational reports to the leadership.
  • Recommend administrative improvements to the National Executive Council.

Accountability

The Executive Director shall remain accountable to the President and the National Executive Council and shall perform all executive functions in accordance with this Constitution.

Article IX — National Executive Council

The National Executive Council shall serve as the principal collective body for organisational coordination, strategic planning and constitutional administration of the Party.

Composition

The Council shall consist of the President, Vice-President, Executive Director, Deputy Chairperson, Secretary-General, Chief Whip, Media Director and Director of Administration.

Functions

  • Frame organisational policies and strategic priorities.
  • Approve major Party initiatives and programmes.
  • Review organisational performance.
  • Coordinate national campaigns and major activities.
  • Review reports submitted by constitutional offices.
  • Ensure compliance with the Constitution.
  • Resolve matters referred to the Council by constitutional offices.
  • Promote coordination and unity across the Party.

Collective Responsibility

All members of the National Executive Council shall exercise their responsibilities in accordance with this Constitution and shall remain accountable for decisions taken collectively by the Council.

Article X — Deputy Chairperson

The Deputy Chairperson shall assist the Executive Leadership in coordinating organisational affairs and ensuring the effective implementation of Party activities.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Support executive meetings and organisational proceedings.
  • Coordinate committees and working groups.
  • Assist in policy implementation.
  • Monitor organisational progress and report significant issues.
  • Support coordination between Party departments.
  • Assist the Executive Director in implementing approved programmes.
  • Perform duties assigned by the Executive Leadership.

Article XI — Secretary-General

The Secretary-General shall serve as the principal administrative and coordinating officer responsible for maintaining official records and ensuring effective communication throughout the Party.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Maintain official Party records.
  • Prepare agendas and minutes of official meetings.
  • Issue official notices and communications.
  • Coordinate Party correspondence.
  • Maintain constitutional archives.
  • Coordinate official documentation between Party offices.
  • Maintain an organised record of constitutional decisions.
  • Assist in ensuring timely implementation of official directives.

Article XII — Chief Whip

The Chief Whip shall be responsible for maintaining Party discipline, constitutional order and effective coordination among Party members and representatives.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Maintain Party discipline.
  • Ensure compliance with the Constitution and Party rules.
  • Promote professionalism and ethical conduct.
  • Assist in resolving internal disputes.
  • Coordinate members on official Party decisions and proceedings.
  • Monitor adherence to authorised Party positions.
  • Report serious disciplinary matters to the Executive Leadership.
  • Promote unity and responsible conduct among Party members.

Article XIII — Media Director

The Media Director shall supervise the Party's public communication, official publications, media relations and digital outreach.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Manage official Party announcements.
  • Supervise official social media platforms.
  • Coordinate media campaigns and public communications.
  • Coordinate communication with media organisations.
  • Protect the Party's public image and reputation.
  • Ensure factual, responsible and consistent communication.
  • Supervise official digital publications.
  • Coordinate public information initiatives approved by the leadership.

Official Communication

Official statements representing the Party shall be issued or authorised through the appropriate constitutional authority and shall remain consistent with this Constitution.

Article XIV — Director of Administration

The Director of Administration shall supervise the Party's administrative infrastructure, operational systems and organisational management.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Manage Party administration and internal operations.
  • Supervise authorised administrative staff and support personnel.
  • Maintain organisational records and operational documentation.
  • Protect Party resources and administrative assets.
  • Implement administrative policies approved by the Leadership.
  • Coordinate logistical arrangements for Party activities.
  • Support efficient functioning of Party departments.
  • Submit administrative reports to the Executive Leadership.

Administrative Accountability

The Director of Administration shall exercise administrative powers within the limits of this Constitution and shall remain accountable to the Executive Director and the National Executive Council.

Article XV — Meetings

Regular Meetings

Regular meetings shall be held periodically to review organisational progress, discuss national issues and strengthen coordination between constitutional offices.

Special Meetings

The President or Executive Director may convene a special meeting whenever urgent constitutional or organisational matters require immediate attention.

Quorum

No constitutional decision shall be valid unless at least one-half of the eligible constitutional officers are present, unless otherwise provided by this Constitution.

Article XVI — Elections

Elections for constitutional offices designated as elective shall be conducted through a free, fair and democratic process.

  • Every eligible member shall possess one equal vote.
  • Voting shall remain confidential.
  • Election schedules shall be officially announced.
  • The candidate receiving the highest valid votes shall be declared elected.
  • The Election Committee shall ensure transparency and impartiality.

Article XVII — Code of Conduct

  • Treat every member with dignity and respect.
  • Promote unity, cooperation and responsible public conduct.
  • Reject discrimination based on religion, caste, language, gender or region.
  • Maintain honesty and integrity in all Party activities.
  • Respect democratic institutions and constitutional values.
  • Use official Party platforms responsibly.
  • Protect confidential Party information.
  • Represent APP with professionalism and discipline.

Article XVIII — Membership

Membership of Awam Pragati Party shall be open to individuals who believe in democratic values, constitutional principles and the objectives of the Party.

Rights of Members

  • Participate in Party activities and discussions.
  • Express opinions on organisational matters.
  • Vote in elections where eligible.
  • Receive equal treatment without discrimination.
  • Access Party information according to constitutional rules.

Duties of Members

  • Respect the Constitution of the Party.
  • Maintain discipline and unity.
  • Support democratic decision-making.
  • Act responsibly while representing the Party.

Article XIX — Committees

The Party may establish committees whenever required for effective administration, policy development and organisational growth.

Functions of Committees

  • Study and recommend policies.
  • Assist constitutional offices.
  • Improve organisational efficiency.
  • Submit reports to the appropriate leadership authority.
  • Support democratic participation within the Party.

Article XX — Finance and Resources

The Party's financial resources shall be managed responsibly, transparently and in accordance with approved organisational rules.

Financial Principles

  • Maintain accurate financial records.
  • Ensure responsible use of Party resources.
  • Prevent misuse of funds and assets.
  • Promote transparency in financial matters.
  • Conduct reviews whenever required.

Article XXI — Amendments

This Constitution may be amended whenever necessary to improve the organisation and strengthen its democratic structure.

  • Amendments shall require proper proposal and discussion.
  • Changes must be approved through the constitutional process.
  • No amendment shall violate democratic principles.
  • The updated Constitution shall be officially recorded.

Article XXII — Interpretation of the Constitution

The interpretation of this Constitution shall be carried out by the appropriate constitutional authority while respecting its original principles and objectives.

  • Interpretations shall remain consistent with constitutional values.
  • Disputes regarding interpretation shall be resolved fairly.
  • No interpretation shall override the fundamental principles of the Party.

Article XXIII — Dissolution

The dissolution of the Party shall only occur through a constitutional decision made according to established democratic procedures.

  • The decision must follow due process.
  • Members shall be informed officially.
  • Remaining resources shall be handled responsibly.

Article XXIV — Final Declaration

Awam Pragati Party shall remain committed to democratic values, constitutional governance, public welfare and responsible leadership.

This Constitution represents the guiding framework of the Party and shall serve as the foundation for its organisation, conduct and future development.

Article XXV — Election Committee

An independent Election Committee may be established to conduct and supervise elections for constitutional offices in a fair, transparent and impartial manner.

Functions

  • Announce official election schedules.
  • Verify eligibility of candidates and voters.
  • Supervise voting procedures.
  • Maintain election records.
  • Declare results after verification.
  • Receive and review election-related complaints.

Article XXVI — Disciplinary Procedure

Any allegation of serious misconduct by a Party member or constitutional office-holder shall be handled through a fair and documented disciplinary procedure.

Principles

  • Every member shall be informed of serious allegations made against them.
  • The concerned member shall be given a reasonable opportunity to respond.
  • Disciplinary decisions shall be based on established Party rules.
  • Serious disciplinary action shall be properly documented.
  • Disciplinary proceedings shall respect the Constitution and principles of fairness.

Article XXVII — Policy & Research

The Party may establish a Policy & Research Division to support evidence-based policymaking, research and long-term organisational planning.

Functions

  • Conduct research on public policy and national issues.
  • Prepare policy proposals for consideration by the leadership.
  • Study social, economic, technological and environmental developments.
  • Support evidence-based decision-making.
  • Prepare reports and recommendations for the National Executive Council.

Article XXVIII — Public Grievances & Suggestions

The Party may establish mechanisms through which members and members of the public may submit suggestions, concerns and proposals relating to the objectives and activities of the Party.

Principles

  • Submissions shall be treated respectfully.
  • Relevant matters may be referred to the appropriate Party office.
  • Constructive suggestions may be considered during policy discussions.
  • Personal information submitted through official channels shall be handled responsibly.
  • Responses shall be provided whenever reasonably possible.

Article XXIX — Youth & Community Participation

The Party may establish youth, community and volunteer organisations to encourage democratic participation and constructive public service.

Functions

  • Encourage participation of young members.
  • Support community-oriented initiatives.
  • Promote civic awareness and constitutional values.
  • Assist with lawful public-service programmes.
  • Provide opportunities for members to develop leadership skills.

Article XXX — Oath of Office

Every person assuming a constitutional office of the Awam Pragati Party shall affirm their commitment to uphold this Constitution, serve the organisation responsibly and act with integrity.

Constitutional Commitment

The office-holder shall faithfully perform the duties of their office, respect democratic procedures, protect the integrity of the Party and exercise their authority only within the limits established by this Constitution.

Article XXXI — Transparency & Accountability

The Awam Pragati Party shall promote transparency, accountability and responsible administration in its organisational affairs.

  • Constitutional decisions shall be recorded where appropriate.
  • Office-holders shall remain accountable for the responsibilities assigned to them.
  • Party resources shall be used responsibly.
  • Official records shall be maintained in an organised manner.
  • Members shall have access to Party information according to constitutional rules.

Article XXXII — Succession & Continuity

The Party shall maintain constitutional continuity whenever a constitutional office becomes temporarily or permanently vacant.

  • Temporary vacancies shall be managed according to the authority assigned by this Constitution.
  • Permanent vacancies shall be filled through the applicable constitutional procedure.
  • No temporary arrangement shall permanently alter the constitutional structure of the Party.
  • All succession procedures shall respect democratic principles and organisational continuity.
