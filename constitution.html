Constitution of
Awam Pragati Party

The supreme governing document of Awam Pragati Party, establishing its principles, structure, rights, responsibilities and commitment to democratic values, national unity, transparency and public service.

Read Constitution

Preamble

We, the members of Awam Pragati Party (APP), unite with the purpose of promoting democratic participation, responsible public service, constitutional governance, social harmony, transparency, accountability and sustainable national development.

This Constitution establishes the principles, organisational structure, rights and responsibilities that shall guide the Party in pursuing its objectives while respecting the Constitution of India and the rule of law.

Articles

Article I — Name and Identity

Section 1 — Official Name

The official name of the organisation shall be Awam Pragati Party (APP).

Section 2 — Purpose

APP exists to encourage democratic participation, public service, constitutional values and inclusive national progress.

Section 3 — Official Emblem

The Party may adopt an official emblem, flag and visual identity as approved by the National Executive.

Article II — Guiding Principles

  • Respect for the Constitution of India.
  • Commitment to democracy and the rule of law.
  • Equality and equal opportunity.
  • Transparency and accountability.
  • Peaceful and constructive public dialogue.
  • Integrity in public life.
  • Inclusive and sustainable development.

Article III — Objectives

  • Promote public participation in democracy.
  • Encourage education and civic awareness.
  • Support economic growth and employment.
  • Strengthen social harmony and national unity.
  • Advance innovation and sustainable development.
  • Promote transparent and accountable governance.

Article IV — Membership

Eligibility

  • Accept the Party Constitution.
  • Follow the Party Code of Conduct.
  • Respect democratic values and fellow members.
  • Support the objectives of APP.

Rights

  • Participate in Party activities.
  • Vote where eligible.
  • Submit proposals and recommendations.
  • Receive equal treatment.

Article V — National Leadership

The Party shall be administered through a National Leadership Council consisting of offices established under this Constitution. Their powers, responsibilities and terms of office shall be defined by subsequent articles.

Article VI — President

The President shall serve as the highest constitutional authority of Awam Pragati Party and shall provide overall leadership, strategic direction and guidance to the organisation.

Powers & Responsibilities

  • Preside over meetings of the National Leadership Council.
  • Represent the Party before public institutions and organisations.
  • Approve major constitutional and organisational decisions.
  • Protect the Constitution, vision and values of APP.
  • Promote unity, integrity and discipline throughout the Party.
  • Issue official directives consistent with this Constitution.

Article VII — Vice-President

The Vice-President shall assist the President in carrying out executive responsibilities and shall act on behalf of the President whenever authorised or when the office is temporarily vacant.

Responsibilities

  • Assist in administration and organisational planning.
  • Coordinate activities between Party departments.
  • Support implementation of national programmes.
  • Chair meetings when authorised by the President.
  • Carry out additional responsibilities assigned by the President.

Article VIII — Executive Director

The Executive Director shall serve as the chief executive officer of the Party and shall supervise the day-to-day administration of APP.

Election

The Executive Director shall be elected by eligible Party members through a free, fair and transparent election conducted every six months. A person may serve a maximum of two consecutive terms before becoming eligible again after one full term has passed.

Responsibilities

  • Implement decisions of the National Leadership Council.
  • Coordinate all organisational departments.
  • Supervise daily administration.
  • Present organisational reports to the leadership.
  • Ensure effective implementation of Party programmes.

Article IX — National Executive Council

The National Executive Council shall consist of the President, Vice-President, Executive Director and other constitutional officers as defined by this Constitution.

Functions

  • Develop organisational policy.
  • Approve strategic initiatives.
  • Coordinate national programmes.
  • Monitor implementation of Party decisions.
  • Review organisational performance.

Article X — Constitutional Offices

The following constitutional offices may be established by the Party to ensure effective administration.

  1. President
  2. Vice-President
  3. Executive Director
  4. Deputy Chairperson
  5. Secretary-General
  6. Chief Whip
  7. Media Director
  8. Director of Administration

Additional offices may be created whenever approved under the amendment procedures of this Constitution.

Article XI — Deputy Chairperson

The Deputy Chairperson shall assist the Executive Leadership in coordinating organisational affairs and ensuring the smooth functioning of Party activities.

Responsibilities

  • Support executive meetings and organisational planning.
  • Coordinate internal committees and working groups.
  • Assist in implementing Party programmes.
  • Monitor progress of assigned initiatives.
  • Perform duties assigned by the Executive Leadership.

Article XII — Secretary-General

The Secretary-General shall serve as the principal administrative officer of the Party and shall be responsible for maintaining official records and organisational communication.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain official documents and records.
  • Prepare agendas and minutes of meetings.
  • Issue official notices and communications.
  • Maintain Party archives and correspondence.
  • Coordinate communication between Party offices.

Article XIII — Chief Whip

The Chief Whip shall promote discipline, coordination and adherence to the Constitution and Code of Conduct within the Party.

Responsibilities

  • Promote organisational discipline.
  • Encourage cooperation among members.
  • Assist in resolving internal disputes.
  • Monitor compliance with Party policies.
  • Report disciplinary matters to the Executive Leadership.

Article XIV — Media Director

The Media Director shall oversee the Party's public communication, official publications and digital outreach.

Responsibilities

  • Manage official announcements.
  • Supervise social media platforms.
  • Coordinate media campaigns and publications.
  • Protect the Party's public image.
  • Ensure that official communications are accurate and responsible.

Article XV — Director of Administration

The Director of Administration shall supervise the Party's operational and administrative functions and ensure the efficient management of resources and organisational systems.

Responsibilities

  • Manage administrative operations.
  • Supervise official platforms and infrastructure.
  • Coordinate administrative staff.
  • Maintain organisational security and records.
  • Support implementation of approved policies.

Article XVI — Meetings & Decision Making

Regular Meetings

The National Leadership Council shall meet periodically to review Party activities, evaluate progress and discuss future plans.

Special Meetings

The President or the Executive Director may convene a special meeting whenever urgent organisational matters require immediate attention.

Quorum

Unless otherwise provided by this Constitution, decisions shall require the presence of at least one-half of the eligible members of the National Leadership Council.

Voting

Every eligible member participating in a meeting shall have one vote. Except where this Constitution requires a higher majority, decisions shall be adopted by a simple majority of votes cast.

Article XVII — Elections

The Party shall conduct democratic elections for constitutional offices designated as elective under this Constitution.

  • Every eligible member shall have one equal vote.
  • Voting shall be conducted fairly, transparently and impartially.
  • Election schedules shall be announced in advance.
  • The candidate receiving the highest number of valid votes shall be declared elected unless otherwise provided by this Constitution.
  • Election procedures shall uphold integrity, secrecy of the ballot and equal opportunity.

Article XVIII — Code of Conduct

  • Treat every member with dignity and respect.
  • Promote unity, cooperation and constructive dialogue.
  • Reject discrimination based on religion, caste, language, gender, ethnicity or region.
  • Maintain honesty and integrity in all Party activities.
  • Respect democratic institutions and constitutional values.
  • Use official Party platforms responsibly.
  • Protect confidential Party information.
  • Represent the Party with professionalism and discipline.

Article XIX — Finance

The Party may receive lawful voluntary contributions, membership fees, fundraising income and other legal sources permitted under applicable laws.

  • Financial records shall be maintained accurately.
  • All expenditures shall be properly documented.
  • Financial transparency and accountability shall be observed.
  • Party funds shall only be used for authorised organisational purposes.

Article XX — Constitutional Amendments

This Constitution may be amended whenever necessary to strengthen the organisation and improve its governance.

  • Any amendment may be proposed before the National Executive Council.
  • A two-thirds majority of eligible voting members shall be required for approval.
  • An adopted amendment shall become effective immediately unless otherwise specified.

Article XXI — Constitutional Supremacy

This Constitution shall remain the supreme governing document of the Awam Pragati Party.

Every office, regulation, policy, programme and organisational decision shall remain consistent with the provisions of this Constitution.

Oath of Membership

"I solemnly affirm that I shall faithfully uphold the Constitution of India and the Constitution of the Awam Pragati Party; respect democracy, promote justice, equality, unity and responsible public service; perform my duties with honesty, integrity and impartiality; and work for the progress, welfare and development of the Nation and its people."
